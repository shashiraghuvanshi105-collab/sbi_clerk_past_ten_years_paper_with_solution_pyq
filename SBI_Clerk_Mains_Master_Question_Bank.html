Question #7 // 2024-25 Quantitative Aptitude • Data Sufficiency
Find the speed of the stream. Statement I: Ratio of speed of boat in still water to stream is 5:1. Statement II: The boat travels 48 km downstream in 4 hours.
  1. Statement I alone is sufficient
  2. Statement II alone is sufficient
  3. Either I or II alone is sufficient
  4. Both I and II together are necessary
  5. Neither I nor II is sufficient
Correct Answer: (D) Both I and II together are necessary
Explanation: Statement II gives downstream speed = 12 km/h. Statement I gives ratio (5x + x = 6x = 12 => x = 2 km/h). Both together are necessary.

Question #12 // 2023-24 Quantitative Aptitude • High-Level Arithmetic
A and B started a business. A invested ₹20,000 for 8 months and B invested ₹30,000 for 6 months. Out of total profit of ₹17,000, find B's share:
  1. ₹8,000
  2. ₹8,500
  3. ₹9,000
  4. ₹9,500
  5. ₹10,000
Correct Answer: (C) ₹9,000
Explanation: Ratio = (20000 × 8) : (30000 × 6) = 160000 : 180000 = 8 : 9. B's share = (9/17) × 17000 = ₹9,000.
