### Questions 8 to 12: Error Detection
*Identify the part of the sentence containing a grammatical error. If there is no error, select (E).*

8. **(A)** The committee members was / **(B)** divided in their opinion / **(C)** regarding the implementation of / **(D)** the newly drafted monetary policy. / **(E)** No error
   - *Answer: (A)*  
   *Explanation:* When members of a collective noun act individually or disagree ("divided in their opinion"), plural verb is required: "The committee members **were** divided..."

9. **(A)** Scarcely had the applicant / **(B)** submitted the online credentials / **(C)** than the verification server / **(D)** experienced an unexpected shutdown. / **(E)** No error
   - *Answer: (C)*  
   *Explanation:* "Scarcely... when" is the correct correlative conjunction pair, not "than". Replace "than" with "when".

10. **(A)** Neither the branch manager / **(B)** nor the customer support executives / **(C)** was able to resolve / **(D)** the recurring technical grievance. / **(E)** No error
    - *Answer: (C)*  
    *Explanation:* With "Neither... nor", the verb agrees with the closer subject ("executives", plural). Hence, replace "was" with "**were** able to resolve".

11. **(A)** She is one of the / **(B)** most dedicated employees / **(C)** who has consistently delivered / **(D)** exceptional results this fiscal quarter. / **(E)** No error
    - *Answer: (C)*  
    *Explanation:* The relative pronoun "who" refers to the plural antecedent "employees". Therefore, the verb must be plural: "who **have** consistently delivered".

12. **(A)** Despite of having / **(B)** comprehensive collateral documents, / **(C)** the small business loan / **(D)** was summarily rejected by the credit officer. / **(E)** No error

### Questions 46 to 50: Quadratic Equations
*Compare quantities $x$ and $y$ and determine the correct relationship.*

46. **I. $x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0$  |  II. $y^2 - 9y + 20 = 0$**
    - Roots for $x$: $(x-3)(x-4) = 0 \implies x = 3, 4$
    - Roots for $y$: $(y-4)(y-5) = 0 \implies y = 4, 5$
    - Comparison: $3 < 4, 3 < 5$; $4 = 4, 4 < 5$.
    - Relationship: **$x \leq y$**

47. **I. $x^2 + 8x + 15 = 0$  |  II. $y^2 + 11y + 30 = 0$**
    - Roots for $x$: $(x+3)(x+5) = 0 \implies x = -3, -5$
    - Roots for $y$: $(y+5)(y+6) = 0 \implies y = -5, -6$
    - Comparison: $-3 > -5, -3 > -6$; $-5 = -5, -5 > -6$.
    - Relationship: **$x \geq y$**

48. **I. $2x^2 - 9x + 10 = 0$  |  II. $2y^2 - 13y + 21 = 0$**
    - For $x$: $2x^2 - 4x - 5x + 10 = 0 \implies x = 2, 2.5$
    - For $y$: $2y^2 - 6y - 7y + 21 = 0 \implies y = 3, 3.5$
    - Relationship: **$x < y$**

49. **I. $x^2 = 64$  |  II. $y = \sqrt{64}$**
    - $x = +8, -8$
    - $y = +8$ (Principal square root is non-negative)
    - Relationship: **$x \leq y$**

50. **I. $x^2 - 11x + 30 = 0$  |  II. $y^2 - 13y + 42 = 0$**

### Questions 56 to 65: Arithmetic Word Problems

56. **A sum invested at 10% per annum compound interest compounded annually yields ₹1,260 as interest after 2 years. Find the principal sum.**
    - Net CI for 2 years at 10% $= 10 + 10 + \frac{100}{100} = 21\%$
    - $21\% \text{ of } P = 1260 \implies P = \frac{1260}{21} \times 100 = \mathbf{₹6,000}$

57. **A and B can complete a work in 12 days and 18 days respectively. They work together for 4 days, then A leaves. In how many days will B finish the remaining work?**
    - Total work $= \text{LCM}(12, 18) = 36 \text{ units}$
    - Efficiency: $A = 3 \text{ units/day}, B = 2 \text{ units/day}$; Combined $= 5 \text{ units/day}$
    - 4 days work $= 4 \times 5 = 20 \text{ units}$; Remaining $= 36 - 20 = 16 \text{ units}$
    - Time taken by B alone $= \frac{16}{2} = \mathbf{8 \text{ days}}$

58. **A train 180 meters long crosses an electric pole in 9 seconds. How much time will it take to cross a platform 260 meters long?**
    - Speed of train $= \frac{180}{9} = 20 \text{ m/s}$
    - Total distance to cross platform $= 180 + 260 = 440 \text{ m}$
    - Time $= \frac{440}{20} = \mathbf{22 \text{ seconds}}$

59. **The ratio of present ages of P and Q is 5 : 7. Four years hence, the sum of their ages will be 56 years. What is P's present age?**
    - Let ages be $5x$ and $7x$.
    - Sum four years hence: $(5x + 4) + (7x + 4) = 56 \implies 12x + 8 = 56 \implies 12x = 48 \implies x = 4$
    - P's present age $= 5 \times 4 = \mathbf{20 \text{ years}}$

60. **A shopkeeper marks an article 40% above cost price and allows a discount of 20% on the marked price. If his profit is ₹48, find the cost price.**
    - Let CP $= 100x \implies \text{MP} = 140x$
    - $\text{SP} = 140x \times 0.80 = 112x$
    - Profit $= 112x - 100x = 12x = 48 \implies x = 4$
    - Cost Price $= 100 \times 4 = \mathbf{₹400}$

61. **A boat travels 36 km downstream in 2 hours and 24 km upstream in 3 hours. Find the speed of the stream.**
    - Downstream speed $v_d = \frac{36}{2} = 18 \text{ km/h}$
    - Upstream speed $v_u = \frac{24}{3} = 8 \text{ km/h}$
    - Speed of stream $= \frac{v_d - v_u}{2} = \frac{18 - 8}{2} = \mathbf{5 \text{ km/h}}$

62. **A container has 80 liters of pure milk. 16 liters of milk is taken out and replaced with water. What is the ratio of milk to water in the final mixture?**
    - Remaining milk $= 80 - 16 = 64 \text{ liters}$
    - Added water $= 16 \text{ liters}$
    - Ratio $= 64 : 16 = \mathbf{4 : 1}$

63. **The average weight of 24 students in a class is 45 kg. If the teacher's weight is included, the average increases by 1 kg. What is the teacher's weight?**
    - New total persons $= 25$; New average $= 46 \text{ kg}$
    - Weight of teacher $= 45 + (25 \times 1) = 45 + 25 = \mathbf{70 \text{ kg}}$

64. **Two pipes X and Y can fill a tank in 15 hours and 20 hours respectively. If both pipes are opened together, how long will it take to fill the tank?**
    - Capacity $= 60 \text{ units}$; Rate of X $= 4$, Rate of Y $= 3$; Combined $= 7 \text{ units/hour}$
    - Time $= \frac{60}{7} = \mathbf{8 \frac{4}{7} \text{ hours}}$

65. **In a bag, there are 4 red balls, 5 blue balls, and 3 green balls. If two balls are drawn at random, what is the probability that both are blue?**
    - Total balls $= 4 + 5 + 3 = 12$
    - Favorable outcomes $= \binom{5}{2} = \frac{5 \times 4}{2} = 10$
    - Total outcomes $= \binom{12}{2} = \frac{12 \times 11}{2} = 66$
    - Probability $= \frac{10}{66} = \mathbf{\frac{5}{33}}$

### Questions 81 to 85: Inequalities
*Given statements with relation symbols ($>, \geq, =, \leq, <$). Mark conclusions.*

81. **Statements:** $M \geq N > O = P \geq Q$
    **Conclusions:** I. $M > P$  |  II. $N \geq Q$
    - $M > P$ is True ($M \geq N > O = P \implies M > P$).
    - $N > Q$ is true, so $N \geq Q$ as a strict certainty is False.
    - *Answer: Only I follows.*

82. **Statements:** $A < B \leq C; C = D < E$
    **Conclusions:** I. $A < D$  |  II. $B < E$
    - $A < B \leq C = D \implies A < D$ (True).
    - $B \leq C = D < E \implies B < E$ (True).
    - *Answer: Both I and II follow.*

83. **Statements:** $W \geq X; X = Y < Z$
    **Conclusions:** I. $W \geq Z$  |  II. $W < Z$
    - $W \geq X = Y < Z$. Opposite signs between $W$ and $Z$.
    - Either $W \geq Z$ or $W < Z$ covers all possibilities!
    - *Answer: Either I or II follows.*

84. **Statements:** $K > L \geq M = N \leq O < P$
    **Conclusions:** I. $K > N$  |  II. $L \geq O$
    - $K > L \geq M = N \implies K > N$ (True).
    - Between $L$ and $O$, opposite signs exist ($M = N \leq O$). False.
    - *Answer: Only I follows.*

85. **Statements:** $R \leq S < T; T = U \leq V$
    **Conclusions:** I. $R < V$  |  II. $S < U$
    - $R \leq S < T = U \leq V \implies R < V$ (True).
    - $S < T = U \implies S < U$ (True).
    - *Answer: Both I and II follow.*

91. **A courier agent walks 10 meters North from a bank branch, turns right and walks 6 meters. He then turns right again and walks 18 meters, before turning right and walking 6 meters to deliver a parcel. How far and in which direction is he now from the bank branch?**
    - North $+10$, East $+6$, South $-18$, West $-6$.

    - Net horizontal displacement $= 6 - 6 = 0$.
    - *Answer: 8 meters South of the branch.*
