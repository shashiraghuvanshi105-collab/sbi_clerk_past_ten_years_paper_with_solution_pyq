# SBI Clerk (Junior Associates) Main Examination 2016
## Solved Memory-Based Question Paper

**Notification / Advt No.:** CRPD/CR/2016-17/01  
**Exam Date:** June 25 & 26, 2016  
**Shift:** Shift 1  
**Total Questions:** 190 | **Maximum Marks:** 200  
**Time Duration:** 160 Minutes  
**Note:** Inaugural 2-Tier CBT Main Examination.

---

# SECTION 1: GENERAL & FINANCIAL AWARENESS (50 Questions / 50 Marks)
- Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) premium rates.
- Unified Payments Interface (UPI) launch by NPCI.
- Small Finance Banks and Payment Banks initial licenses granted by RBI.
- Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) constitution under RBI Act amendment.
- Marginal Cost of Funds based Lending Rate (MCLR) introduction replacing base rate.

# SECTION 2: GENERAL ENGLISH (40 Questions / 40 Marks)
- Reading Comprehension on financial inclusion and mobile banking.
- Classic Cloze Test, error spotting, and para jumbles.

# SECTION 3: QUANTITATIVE APTITUDE (50 Questions / 50 Marks)
- Tabular DI, Bar Graph, and Line Graph DI.
- Data Sufficiency and Arithmetic Word Problems.

# SECTION 4: REASONING & COMPUTER APTITUDE (50 Questions / 60 Marks)
- Circular and linear seating arrangements.
- Floor puzzle and blood relations.
- Input-Output machine and logical reasoning.
- Computer memory, operating systems, and network topologies.
