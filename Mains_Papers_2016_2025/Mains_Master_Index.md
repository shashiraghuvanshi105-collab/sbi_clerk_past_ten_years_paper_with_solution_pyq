### 2. SBI Clerk Main Examination Structure & Marking Scheme

| Section | Test Medium | No. of Questions | Maximum Marks | Sectional Duration |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **1. General / Financial Awareness** | Bilingual | 50 | 50 | 35 Minutes |
| **2. General English** | English | 40 | 40 | 35 Minutes |
| **3. Quantitative Aptitude** | Bilingual | 50 | 50 | 45 Minutes |
| **4. Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude** | Bilingual | 50 | 60 | 45 Minutes |
| **TOTAL** | — | **190** | **200** | **2 Hours 40 Minutes (160 Minutes)** |

> **Key Exam Rules:**
> - **Negative Marking:** 0.25 (1/4th) of assigned marks deducted per wrong answer. In the Reasoning section, questions carrying 1.5 marks attract $-0.375$ penalty.
> - **Sectional Cut-offs:** SBI **does not** enforce sectional cutoffs in Mains. Candidate merit is determined strictly on the aggregate score out of 200 marks.
> - **Final Merit List:** Selection is based 100% on the Phase 2 Mains score (Prelims is purely qualifying).

### 3. Subject-Wise In-Depth Syllabus & Topic Weightage

#### A. General / Financial Awareness (50 Questions / 50 Marks — 35 Mins)
- **Current Banking & Financial Affairs:** 20–25 Qs (RBI circulars, repo/reverse repo/SDF/MSF rates, priority sector lending norms, bank mergers, PCA framework, CBDC / Digital Rupee developments).
- **National & International Current Affairs:** 15–18 Qs (Government welfare schemes, MoUs, multilateral summits, rankings, sports, summits, awards, defence exercises).
- **Static Banking & Financial Terminologies:** 6–8 Qs (Negotiable Instruments Act, DICGC insurance limit, Basel III norms, CRR & SLR definitions, capital markets, SEBI/PFRDA/IRDAI regulators).
- **Static GK:** 3–5 Qs (National parks, wildlife sanctuaries, dams, headquarters of international agencies, nuclear power plants).

#### B. General English (40 Questions / 40 Marks — 35 Mins)
- **Advanced Reading Comprehension (2 Sets):** 12–15 Qs (Global economics, financial policy, technological displacement, environmental governance). Includes inference, vocab in context, author's attitude, and tone questions.
- **Sentence Connectors & Starters:** 4–5 Qs.
- **Match the Columns (Column I vs Column II):** 4–5 Qs.
- **Error Detection / Sentence Correction (Multi-sentence pattern):** 5 Qs.
- **Cloze Test / Double Fillers:** 5–6 Qs.
- **Word Usage & Idiomatic Expressions:** 4–5 Qs.

#### C. Quantitative Aptitude (50 Questions / 50 Marks — 45 Mins)
- **High-Level Data Interpretation (3 to 4 Sets):** 15–20 Qs:
  - Missing Table DI
  - Radar / Spider DI
  - Caselet DI (Venn diagram or arithmetic based)
  - Funnel / Double Mixed DI (e.g., Pie Chart + Bar Graph)
- **Data Sufficiency (DS):** 5 Qs (Two-statement or three-statement sufficiency).
- **Quantity Comparison (Quantity I vs Quantity II vs Quantity III):** 5 Qs.
- **High-Yield Arithmetic Word Problems:** 15–20 Qs (Time & Work with efficiency alterations, Pipes & Cisterns with leakages, Compound Interest compounded half-yearly/quarterly, Boats & Streams, Speed Time & Distance relative velocity, Probability & Permutations, Mensuration 3D).

#### D. Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude (50 Questions / 60 Marks — 45 Mins)
- **High-Difficulty Puzzles & Seating Arrangements:** 20–25 Qs (4 to 5 sets):
  - Concentric Circular Seating (inner circle facing out, outer facing in).
  - Multi-variable Floor & Flat Puzzle (floors, flats, and color/car variables).
  - Year-Age Matrix Puzzle with base year calculations.
  - Uncertain Number of Persons in a Row.
- **Machine Input-Output:** 5 Qs (Step-by-step alphanumeric rearrangement or shifting logic).
- **Coded Reasoning:** 8–10 Qs:
  - Coded Inequalities ($A @ B \implies A > B$).
  - Coded Direction & Distance ($P \# Q(12m) \implies P$ is north of $Q$).
  - Coded Blood Relations ($M \times N \implies M$ is mother of $N$).
- **Critical / Logical Reasoning:** 7–10 Qs (Statement & Assumption, Cause & Effect, Course of Action, Strengthening/Weakening Arguments).
- **Computer Aptitude:** 3–5 Qs (Binary to Decimal conversion, flowchart logic, memory hierarchy).
