# SECTION 1: GENERAL & FINANCIAL AWARENESS (50 Questions / 50 Marks)

1. **What is the authorized capital of the National Asset Reconstruction Company Limited (NARCL)?**  
   - *Answer:* ₹2,000 crore (Government backstop guarantee of ₹30,600 crore).

2. **Under the PM Vishwakarma Scheme, what is the maximum concessional loan provided to traditional artisans in the first tranche?**  
   - *Answer:* ₹1,00,000 at a subsidized interest rate of 5%.

3. **In foreign exchange management, what is the full form of 'Vostro Account'?**  
   - *Answer:* An account held by a foreign bank with a domestic bank in the domestic bank's currency ("yours with us").

4. **What is the current threshold for mandatory implementation of the dynamic QR code on B2C invoices under GST?**  
   - *Answer:* Entities with aggregate turnover exceeding ₹500 crore.

5. **Which agency regulates the payment aggregator ecosystem in India?**  
   - *Answer:* Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

6. **What is the interest subvention offered under the Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS) for short-term crop loans up to ₹3 lakh?**  
   - *Answer:* 1.5% per annum to lending institutions.

7. **What is the primary mandate of the Central KYC Registry (CKYCR)?**  
   - *Answer:* Centralizing KYC records of customers across the entire financial sector to avoid repetitive KYC verifications.

8. **Which public sector lender merged with State Bank of India in the historic 2017 amalgamation?**  
   - *Answer:* State Bank of Bikaner and Jaipur, State Bank of Hyderabad, State Bank of Mysore, State Bank of Patiala, State Bank of Travancore, and Bharatiya Mahila Bank.

9. **What is the maximum limit on overdraft in PMJDY accounts for senior citizens up to 65 years?**  
   - *Answer:* ₹10,000.

10. **Under Basel III norms, what is the Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB) requirement for Indian banks?**  
    - *Answer:* 2.5% of Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital.

*(Questions 11 to 50 cover G20 declarations, Union Budget allocations, RBI monetary policy stance, and economic indicators).*
