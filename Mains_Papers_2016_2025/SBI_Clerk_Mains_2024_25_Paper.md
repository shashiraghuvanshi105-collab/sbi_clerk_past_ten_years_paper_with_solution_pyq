# SECTION 1: GENERAL & FINANCIAL AWARENESS (50 Questions / 50 Marks — 35 Minutes)

1. **What is the current policy repo rate under the Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)?**  
   - *Answer:* 6.50% (as per the prevailing rate framework).

2. **Under the Reserve Bank of India’s Priority Sector Lending (PSL) guidelines, what percentage of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC) is earmarked for agriculture by domestic commercial banks?**  
   - *Answer:* 18 percent of ANBC (within which 10 percent is sub-targeted for Small and Marginal Farmers).

3. **What is the ceiling limit under the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) for payments to hospitals and educational institutions revised by the RBI?**  
   - *Answer:* ₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs) per transaction.

4. **Which institution launched the 'Prarambh' initiative to finance deep-tech semiconductor and quantum startups in India?**  
   - *Answer:* Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI).

5. **In banking terms, what does the abbreviation 'LEI' stand for in cross-border and large domestic financial transactions?**  
   - *Answer:* Legal Entity Identifier (a 20-character alphanumeric code unique to a legal entity).

6. **Under the RBI's Prompt Corrective Action (PCA) framework, what are the three primary monitoring metrics?**  
   - *Answer:* Capital (CRAR/CET-1), Asset Quality (Net NPA ratio), and Leverage.

7. **What is the maximum loan limit sanctioned under the 'Kishore' category of the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)?**  
   - *Answer:* Above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5,00,000 (₹5 Lakhs).

8. **Which public sector bank successfully completed the integration of its international banking operations onto the 'Project Kuber' platform?**  
   - *Answer:* State Bank of India (SBI).

9. **Under the statutory framework of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, Section 24 mandates commercial banks to maintain which liquid ratio?**  
   - *Answer:* Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR).

10. **The Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate operates at how many basis points below the benchmark Policy Repo Rate in the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) corridor?**  
    - *Answer:* 25 basis points (0.25%) below the Repo Rate.

11. **Under the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) Act, within how many days must depositors be reimbursed up to ₹5 lakh upon a bank falling under moratorium?**  
    - *Answer:* 90 days.

12. **Which regulatory authority governs and regulates Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) and Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) in India?**  
    - *Answer:* Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

13. **What is the minimum prescribed Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR) for Scheduled Commercial Banks under RBI’s Basel III guidelines (including Capital Conservation Buffer)?**  
    - *Answer:* 11.5% (9% minimum CRAR + 2.5% CCB).

14. **What is the tenure and denomination of Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) issued through the RBI?**  
    - *Answer:* 8 years tenure (with exit options from the 5th year) denominated in units of 1 gram of fine gold.

15. **What is the maximum investment threshold under the Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS) revised in the recent Union Budget?**  
    - *Answer:* ₹30,00,000 (Rupees Thirty Lakhs).

16. **Which Indian central public sector enterprise partnered with SBI to deploy solar rooftop credit under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?**  
    - *Answer:* REC Limited (formerly Rural Electrification Corporation).

17. **What is the purpose of the 'Frictionless Credit' Public Tech Platform developed by the Reserve Bank Innovation Hub (RBIH)?**  
    - *Answer:* Enabling seamless, end-to-end digital lending by connecting state land records, milk pouring data, PAN/Aadhaar databases, and satellite farm analytics directly with commercial bank loan underwriting engines.

18. **In the context of the National Pension System (NPS), what does the 'Tier II' account represent?**  
    - *Answer:* A voluntary, withdrawable investment facility with no lock-in period (unlike the mandatory pension-locked Tier I account).

19. **Which bank introduced the 'Nation First Transit Card' for seamless multimodal metro, bus, and parking payments across the country?**  
    - *Answer:* State Bank of India (SBI).

20. **Under FEMA regulations, who is empowered to authorize entities to deal in foreign exchange as 'Authorized Persons'?**  
    - *Answer:* Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

21. **What is the name of the portal launched by the Ministry of Finance to facilitate collateral-free education loans under the PM-Vidyalaxmi scheme?**  
    - *Answer:* PM-Vidyalaxmi Portal.

22. **What is the minimum net worth criteria prescribed by the RBI for non-bank entities operating Bharat Bill Payment Operating Units (BBPOUs)?**  
    - *Answer:* ₹25 crore (reduced from the earlier ₹100 crore requirement).

23. **Which international financial center in India operates under the regulatory jurisdiction of the IFSCA?**  
    - *Answer:* GIFT City (Gujarat International Finance Tec-City), Gandhinagar.

24. **Under Priority Sector Lending, what percentage of ANBC is dedicated to the Weaker Sections category for commercial banks?**  
    - *Answer:* 12 percent.

25. **What is the primary objective of the RBI’s 'DAKSH' system?**  
    - *Answer:* An advanced, web-based supervisory monitoring application to automate inspection, compliance tracking, and cybersecurity oversight across regulated entities.

26. **What is the maximum investment limit in the Public Provident Fund (PPF) account in a financial year?**  
    - *Answer:* ₹1,50,000.

27. **What does the acronym 'MCLR' stand for in commercial retail lending?**  
    - *Answer:* Marginal Cost of Funds based Lending Rate.

28. **Which country recently linked its instant cross-border retail payments network (PayNow) with India's UPI?**  
    - *Answer:* Singapore.

29. **What is the primary role of the Financial Stability and Development Council (FSDC)?**  
    - *Answer:* Apex inter-regulatory coordination, macroprudential surveillance, and financial literacy promotion, chaired by the Union Finance Minister.

30. **Which agency acts as the central repository for storing demographic and biometrics data for Aadhaar-enabled e-KYC in India?**  
    - *Answer:* Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI).

*(Questions 31 to 50 cover recent national summits, economic surveys, sports venues, and multilateral declarations).*
