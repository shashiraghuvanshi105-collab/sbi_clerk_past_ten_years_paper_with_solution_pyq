# SBI Clerk (Junior Associates) Main Examination 2020
## Solved Memory-Based Question Paper

**Notification / Advt No.:** CRPD/CR/2019-20/20  
**Exam Date:** October 31 & November 7, 2020  
**Shift:** Shift 1  
**Total Questions:** 190 | **Maximum Marks:** 200  
**Time Duration:** 160 Minutes  

---

# SECTION 1: GENERAL & FINANCIAL AWARENESS (50 Questions / 50 Marks)
- Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme (ECLGS) for MSMEs post-lockdown.
- Special Liquidity Facility (SLF) extended to NABARD, NHB, and SIDBI.
- PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana welfare coverage.
- Restructuring framework for personal and corporate loans.
- Headquarters of international bodies and national tiger reserves.

# SECTION 2: GENERAL ENGLISH (40 Questions / 40 Marks)
- RC 1: Economic impact of global lockdowns and remote work shift.
- RC 2: Renewable energy investment trends.
- Column matching, connectors, vocabulary in context, and cloze test.

# SECTION 3: QUANTITATIVE APTITUDE (50 Questions / 50 Marks)
- Double Pie Chart DI: Production and export of agricultural commodities.
- Caselet DI on COVID-19 relief fund distribution.
- Data Sufficiency and high-level arithmetic word problems.

# SECTION 4: REASONING & COMPUTER APTITUDE (50 Questions / 60 Marks)
- Box puzzle with 8 boxes and distinct colored items.
- Square table seating (4 corners facing in, 4 sides facing out).
- Machine Input-Output and Coded Inequalities.
- Critical reasoning questions on public health and monetary interventions.
