## SECTION 1: GENERAL & FINANCIAL AWARENESS (50 Questions / 50 Marks — 35 Mins)

1. **What is the current regulatory framework for Priority Sector Lending (PSL) targets mandated by the RBI for domestic scheduled commercial banks?**  
   - *Answer:* 40 percent of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC) or Credit Equivalent Amount of Off-Balance Sheet Exposure (CEOBE), whichever is higher.

2. **Under the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) Act, what is the maximum insurance coverage provided per depositor per bank in India?**  
   - *Answer:* ₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs), covering both principal and interest held across all deposit accounts (savings, current, fixed, recurring).

3. **In the context of monetary policy operations, what does the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate represent?**  
   - *Answer:* The floor rate of the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) corridor through which the Reserve Bank of India absorbs overnight uncollateralized liquidity from commercial banks.

4. **What is the minimum capital requirement for establishing a Small Finance Bank (SFB) as prescribed by the Reserve Bank of India?**  
   - *Answer:* ₹200 crore (previously ₹100 crore). For Urban Co-operative Banks transitioning to SFBs, the initial requirement is ₹100 crore, to be scaled to ₹200 crore within five years.

5. **Which financial entity manages and operates the National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) and Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) systems?**  
   - *Answer:* Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

6. **What is the validity period of a Cheque / Demand Draft in India according to RBI directives?**  
   - *Answer:* 3 months from the date of issue.

7. **Under the PM Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY), what is the maximum overdraft limit available to eligible account holders after satisfactory account maintenance?**  
   - *Answer:* Up to ₹10,000 (with no conditions up to ₹2,000; upper age limit relaxed to 65 years).

8. **What does the acronym 'CIBIL' stand for, and what score range is considered prime by commercial lending institutions?**  
   - *Answer:* Credit Information Bureau (India) Limited. Score ranges from 300 to 900; a score of 750+ is considered prime for low-risk retail credit sanctioning.

9. **In the Union Budget, what is the fiscal deficit target under the Medium-Term Fiscal Policy Statement?**  
   - *Answer:* Bringing the central government's fiscal deficit below 4.5% of GDP.

10. **Which Indian regulatory authority supervises the functioning of the National Pension System (NPS)?**  
    - *Answer:* Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA).

11. **What is the core distinction between Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) and Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR)?**  
    - *Answer:* CRR is the percentage of Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL) that banks must keep as cash reserves with the RBI (on which no interest is earned), while SLR is the percentage of NDTL maintained with banks themselves in approved liquid assets (government securities, gold, cash).

12. **Which committee recommended the implementation of the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) framework in India?**  
    - *Answer:* Dr. Urjit Patel Committee.

13. **What is the primary function of the National Asset Reconstruction Company Limited (NARCL) or 'Bad Bank'?**  
    - *Answer:* Aggregating and consolidating stressed/non-performing assets (NPAs) of ₹500 crore and above from banking institutions to resolve and liquidate them.

14. **What is the tenure of Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) issued by the RBI on behalf of the Government of India?**  
    - *Answer:* 8 years, with an exit option available after the 5th year on coupon payment dates.

15. **What is the maximum investment allowed under the Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS)?**  
    - *Answer:* ₹30,00,000 (Rupees Thirty Lakhs).

16. **Under Basel III guidelines, what is the minimum prescribed Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio for Indian commercial banks?**  
    - *Answer:* 5.5% of Risk Weighted Assets (plus a Capital Conservation Buffer of 2.5%, making it 8.0%).

17. **Which institution is designated as the primary regulator for housing finance companies (HFCs) in India?**  
    - *Answer:* Reserve Bank of India (transferred from National Housing Bank - NHB).

18. **What is the headquarters location of the Asian Development Bank (ADB)?**  
    - *Answer:* Mandaluyong, Metro Manila, Philippines.

19. **What does the term 'Prompt Corrective Action' (PCA) framework refer to in banking?**  
    - *Answer:* A supervisory mechanism invoked by the RBI against banks whose financial health falls below designated thresholds in capital adequacy, asset quality (Net NPA), and leverage.

20. **Under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016, what is the maximum time limit for completing the Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP)?**  
    - *Answer:* 330 days (including legal extensions and litigation time).

## SECTION 2: GENERAL ENGLISH (40 Questions / 40 Marks — 35 Mins)

### Questions 51 to 58: Reading Comprehension (Mains Analytical RC)
*Context: Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) and their macro-financial implications.*

The introduction of sovereign digital currencies, commonly known as Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs), represents a tectonic shift in the ontology of fiat money. Unlike decentralized cryptocurrencies whose valuations oscillate wildly on market speculation and lack sovereign backing, a CBDC is a direct legal tender liability of the issuing central bank. The Reserve Bank of India’s rollout of the Digital Rupee (e₹) in wholesale and retail pilot projects reflects a multi-pronged ambition: curtailing the colossal logistics expenditure associated with physical currency management, fortifying domestic payment resilience against private monopoly networks, and providing a sovereign rails alternative in an increasingly dematerialized commerce ecosystem.

Nonetheless, macroeconomists urge circumspection regarding bank disintermediation risks. If commercial bank depositors perceive digital cash held directly on central bank ledgers as intrinsically safer during episodes of financial distress, systemic bank runs could accelerate at unprecedented velocity. To attenuate this run risk, central banks are experimenting with two-tier distribution models where commercial lenders retain client custody and payment facilitation roles, as well as applying zero interest rates on retail CBDC balances to discourage idle hoarding.

51. **How does a CBDC fundamentally diverge from decentralized cryptocurrencies according to the passage?**  
    - *Answer:* A CBDC is a direct legal tender liability of the central bank with stable sovereign backing, unlike volatile, unbacked cryptocurrencies.
52. **What does the term "bank disintermediation" imply in this context?**  
    - *Answer:* The migration of retail deposits away from commercial banks directly into central bank accounts, diminishing commercial banks' lending capacity.
53. **What policy measure is highlighted to prevent retail CBDCs from accelerating bank runs?**  
    - *Answer:* Applying a two-tier distribution model and maintaining zero interest rates on retail digital currency balances.
54. **What is the meaning of the word "attenuate" as used in the passage?**  
    - *Answer:* To reduce the force, effect, or severity of something (mitigate/lessen).

## SECTION 3: QUANTITATIVE APTITUDE (50 Questions / 50 Marks — 45 Mins)

### Caselet Data Interpretation (Questions 101 to 105)
*Data:* An investment portfolio consists of 3 asset classes: Equity, Debt, and Gold across two fiscal years 2024 and 2025.
- In 2024, the total corpus was ₹50 Lakhs. The allocation ratio was $\text{Equity} : \text{Debt} : \text{Gold} = 5 : 3 : 2$.
  - $\text{Equity}_{2024} = ₹25 \text{ Lakhs}$
  - $\text{Debt}_{2024} = ₹15 \text{ Lakhs}$
  - $\text{Gold}_{2024} = ₹10 \text{ Lakhs}$
- In 2025, the total corpus increased by 20% to ₹60 Lakhs. Equity allocation increased by 20%, Debt allocation increased by 33.33%, and the remainder was allocated to Gold.
  - $\text{Equity}_{2025} = 25 \times 1.20 = ₹30 \text{ Lakhs}$
  - $\text{Debt}_{2025} = 15 \times (1 + \frac{1}{3}) = ₹20 \text{ Lakhs}$
  - $\text{Gold}_{2025} = 60 - (30 + 20) = ₹10 \text{ Lakhs}$

101. **What is the percentage change in the allocation to Gold from 2024 to 2025?**  
     - $\text{Gold}_{2024} = 10 \text{ Lakhs}, \text{Gold}_{2025} = 10 \text{ Lakhs} \implies \mathbf{0\% \text{ change}}$ (Remained constant).

102. **What is the ratio of Debt allocation in 2025 to Equity allocation in 2024?**  
     - $\text{Debt}_{2025} = 20 \text{ Lakhs}$, $\text{Equity}_{2024} = 25 \text{ Lakhs}$  
     - $\text{Ratio} = 20 : 25 = \mathbf{4 : 5}$.

103. **If Gold appreciated by 15% in 2025, what was the return generated exclusively by Gold in ₹?**  
     - $\text{Return} = 15\% \text{ of } 10 \text{ Lakhs} = \mathbf{₹1,50,000}$.

104. **A boat's speed in still water is 15 km/h. It travels 60 km downstream and 40 km upstream in a total of 7.5 hours. Find the speed of the stream ($s$).**  
     - Equation: $\frac{60}{15 + s} + \frac{40}{15 - s} = 7.5$
     - Testing $s = 5$: $\frac{60}{20} + \frac{40}{10} = 3 + 4 = 7 \text{ hours} \neq 7.5$.
     - Testing $s = \text{solve algebraically}$:  
       $60(15 - s) + 40(15 + s) = 7.5(225 - s^2)$  
       $900 - 60s + 600 + 40s = 1687.5 - 7.5s^2$  
       $1500 - 20s = 1687.5 - 7.5s^2 \implies 7.5s^2 - 20s - 187.5 = 0$  
       Multiply by 2: $15s^2 - 40s - 375 = 0 \implies 3s^2 - 8s - 75 = 0$  
       $3s^2 - 25s + 9s - 75 = 0 \implies (3s - 25)(s + 5) = 0$  
       $s = \frac{25}{3} \text{ km/h} = \mathbf{8.33 \text{ km/h}}$ or checking integer parameters based on specific shift variations.

105. **A container has 120 liters of wine. 20 liters is drawn out and replaced with water. This process is repeated one more time. What is the remaining quantity of wine in the container?**  
     - Formula: $Q = P \left(1 - \frac{x}{C}\right)^n = 120 \left(1 - \frac{20}{120}\right)^2 = 120 \left(\frac{5}{6}\right)^2 = 120 \times \frac{25}{36} = \frac{10 \times 25}{3} = \mathbf{83.33 \text{ liters}}$.
