   - (D) 400

   *Answer:* **(B)**  
   *Step-by-step Solution:*  
   $45\% \text{ of } 800 = 0.45 \times 800 = 360$.

   $360 + 480 - ? = 420 \implies 840 - ? = 420 \implies ? = 840 - 420 = 420$.

   $32 + 36 - 18 = 68 - 18 = 50 \approx 49$ (Approximation) $\implies ? = \sqrt{49} = 7$.

### Directions (Q48 – Q52): Quadratic Equations
*In the following questions, two equations (I) and (II) are given. You have to solve both equations and establish the relationship between x and y.*

48. **I. $x^2 - 11x + 30 = 0$**  
    **II. $y^2 - 13y + 42 = 0$**
   - (A) $x > y$
   - (B) $x < y$
   - (C) $x \ge y$
   - (D) $x \le y$
   - (E) $x = y$ or relationship cannot be established
   *Answer:* **(D)**  
   *Roots:*  
   $x^2 - 11x + 30 = 0 \implies (x - 5)(x - 6) = 0 \implies x = 5, 6$.  
   $y^2 - 13y + 42 = 0 \implies (y - 6)(y - 7) = 0 \implies y = 6, 7$.  
   Comparing: $5 < 6, 5 < 7, 6 = 6, 6 < 7 \implies x \le y$.

49. **I. $x^2 - 7x + 12 = 0$**  
    **II. $y^2 - 9y + 20 = 0$**

   - (B) $x < y$

   - (D) $x \le y$
   - (E) $x = y$ or relationship cannot be established
   *Answer:* **(D)**  
   *Roots:*  
   $x = 3, 4$.  
   $y = 4, 5$.  
   $3 < 4, 3 < 5, 4 = 4, 4 < 5 \implies x \le y$.

   - (D) 16 years
   - (E) 32 years
   *Answer:* **(B)**

   - (A) ₹1800

   - (C) 4.5 days
   - (D) 5 days
   - (E) 6 days
   *Answer:* **(C)**

62. **A boat travels 36 km downstream in 2 hours and takes 3 hours to cover 27 km upstream. Find the speed of the current.**
   - (A) 3 km/h
   - (B) 4 km/h
   - (C) 4.5 km/h
   - (D) 5 km/h
   - (E) 2.5 km/h
   *Answer:* **(C)**  
   *Solution:*  
   Downstream speed $D = \frac{36}{2} = 18 \text{ km/h}$.  
   Upstream speed $U = \frac{27}{3} = 9 \text{ km/h}$.  
   Speed of current $= \frac{D - U}{2} = \frac{18 - 9}{2} = \frac{9}{2} = 4.5 \text{ km/h}$.

63. **A train of length 240 m running at a speed of 72 km/h crosses a platform in 25 seconds. Find the length of the platform.**

   - (D) 280 m
   - (E) 300 m
   *Answer:* **(C)**  
   *Solution:*  
   Speed $= 72 \times \frac{5}{18} = 20 \text{ m/s}$.  
   Total distance covered in $25 \text{ s} = 20 \times 25 = 500 \text{ m}$.  
   Length of platform $= 500 - 240 = 260 \text{ m}$.

64. **A jar contains 80 liters of a mixture of milk and water in the ratio 7 : 1. How much water must be added to make the ratio of milk to water 5 : 1?**
   - (A) 4 liters
   - (B) 5 liters
   - (C) 6 liters
   - (D) 7 liters
   - (E) 8 liters
   *Answer:* **(A)**

   Water $= \frac{1}{8} \times 80 = 10 \text{ liters}$.

   $\frac{70}{10 + w} = \frac{5}{1} \implies 50 + 5w = 70 \implies 5w = 20 \implies w = 4 \text{ liters}$.
