# SECTION 1: ENGLISH LANGUAGE (30 Questions — Composite Time)
- **Q1 – Q10 (Reading Comprehension):** Story/narrative with moral and economic implications. Vocabulary and direct inference questions.
- **Q11 – Q15 (Cloze Test):** Story-based narrative cloze passage.
- **Q16 – Q20 (Spotting Errors):** Classic grammar rules (subject-verb agreement, modifiers, conjunctions).
- **Q21 – Q25 (Para Jumble):** 5-sentence coherent story rearrangement.
- **Q26 – Q30 (Fill in the Blanks):** Single word contextual fillers.

# SECTION 2: NUMERICAL ABILITY (35 Questions — Composite Time)
- **Q31 – Q45 (Simplification):** 15 high-speed simplification questions (fractions, decimals, square roots, percentages).
- **Q46 – Q50 (Number Series):** 5 missing number series questions.
- **Q51 – Q55 (Data Interpretation):** Single table or bar graph with direct ratio, average, and percentage questions.
- **Q56 – Q65 (Arithmetic Word Problems):** 10 classic arithmetic word problems (Simple Interest, Ratio, Average, Time and Work, Train and Distance, Profit and Loss, Boat and Stream, Mensuration).

# SECTION 3: REASONING ABILITY (35 Questions — Composite Time)
- **Q66 – Q70 (Circular Seating):** 8 persons seated around circular table all facing center.
- **Q71 – Q75 (Linear Seating):** 8 persons in a straight line all facing North.
- **Q76 – Q80 (Syllogisms):** 5 traditional syllogism questions (All, Some, No).
- **Q81 – Q85 (Inequalities):** 5 direct inequality questions.
- **Q86 – Q90 (Alphanumeric Series):** 5 questions on letter-number-symbol sequence.
- **Q91 – Q95 (Coding-Decoding):** 5 questions on letter shifting and word substitution.
- **Q96 – Q100 (Blood Relations & Direction Sense):** 3 blood relation questions and 2 direction distance questions.
