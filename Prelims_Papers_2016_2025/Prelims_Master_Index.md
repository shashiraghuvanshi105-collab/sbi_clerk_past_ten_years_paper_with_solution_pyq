### 3. Subject-Wise Topic Weightage Trends (2016–2025)

#### A. English Language (30 Questions)
- **Reading Comprehension (RC):** 8–10 Qs (Economics, technology, environmental issues, banking habits, or story-based). Includes 2–3 vocabulary questions (Synonyms/Antonyms) and inference-based questions.
- **Cloze Test:** 5–6 Qs (Contextual grammar and vocabulary).
- **Error Detection / Spotting:** 5 Qs (Single line sentence divided into 4 parts, or finding the grammatically correct sentence).
- **Sentence Rearrangement / Para Jumbles:** 5 Qs.
- **Word Swap / Word Replacement:** 4–5 Qs (Modern format introduced from 2019 onwards).
- **Fillers (Single / Double):** 4–5 Qs.

#### B. Quantitative Aptitude (35 Questions)
- **Speed Math (Simplification / Approximation):** 10–15 Qs (The primary scoring engine; target solving in 4–6 minutes).
- **Number Series:** 5 Qs (Missing or Wrong Number Series).
- **Quadratic Equations ($ax^2 + bx + c = 0$):** 5 Qs (Comparison of $x$ and $y$).
- **Data Interpretation (DI):** 5–10 Qs (Tabular DI, Bar Graph, Line Graph, or Caselet/Arithmetic DI).
- **Arithmetic Word Problems:** 10–12 Qs (Profit & Loss, SI & CI, Time & Work, Speed Time & Distance, Ratio & Proportion, Partnerships, Ages, Mixture & Allegations, Mensuration).

#### C. Reasoning Ability (35 Questions)
- **Puzzles & Seating Arrangements:** 15–20 Qs (3 to 4 sets of 5 questions each):
  - Linear Seating (Single row facing North, or North-South facing).
  - Circular / Square Seating (Facing Centre or Inside-Outside).
  - Floor & Flat Puzzles (e.g., 4 floors, 2 flats per floor).
  - Box Puzzles (7 or 8 boxes stacked).
  - Month & Date Puzzles (e.g., 4 months, 2 dates each).
  - Designation / Hierarchy Puzzles (MD, GM, DGM, Manager, Clerk).
- **Syllogisms:** 3–5 Qs (Modern "Only a few", "Some are not" patterns).
- **Inequalities:** 3–5 Qs (Direct and coded comparisons).
- **Alphanumeric / Number / Letter Series:** 4–5 Qs.
- **Coding-Decoding:** 4–5 Qs (Chinese/Fictitious language substitution).
- **Direction & Distance:** 3 Qs.
- **Blood Relations:** 3 Qs.
- **Miscellaneous Word / Number Operations:** 2–3 Qs.
