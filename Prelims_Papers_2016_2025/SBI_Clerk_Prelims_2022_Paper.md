### Directions (Q1 – Q8): Reading Comprehension
*Read the following passage carefully:*

The emergence of FinTech unicorns in India has radically redefined the retail consumer experience. Historically, securing an uncollateralized personal loan or credit card required an exhaustive paper trail, multiple physical visits to a bank branch, and weeks of bureaucratic manual verification. Today, open application programming interfaces (APIs), the Account Aggregator (AA) framework, and instant Aadhaar e-KYC have compressed loan sanctioning to under five minutes. Digital-first consumers can apply, receive underwriting approval, and disburse credit directly into their digital wallets from the comfort of their homes.

However, instantaneous credit issuance has ignited concerns regarding reckless personal over-indebtedness, particularly among Gen Z and millennial borrowers. With "Buy Now Pay Later" (BNPL) options seamlessly embedded into online shopping checkouts, frictionless spending has decoupled consumer impulse from affordability. In response, the Reserve Bank of India issued digital lending guidelines mandating that loan disbursements must flow strictly from regulated commercial bank accounts to borrowers without intermediary pass-through accounts, and that all fees must be transparently disclosed via a Key Fact Statement (KFS).

1. **What has dramatically accelerated personal loan sanctioning times in India?**  
   - Open APIs, the Account Aggregator framework, and instant Aadhaar e-KYC. *(Answer: B)*
2. **What concern has arisen from frictionless credit tools like BNPL?**  
   - Reckless personal over-indebtedness among younger demographics. *(Answer: A)*
3. **What did the RBI mandate through its digital lending guidelines?**  
   - Direct fund transfers between regulated bank accounts and transparent fee disclosure via KFS. *(Answer: C)*
4. **Synonym of "EXHAUSTIVE":** Thorough / Comprehensive. *(Answer: B)*
5. **Antonym of "FRICTIONLESS":** Obstructed / Cumbersome. *(Answer: D)*
6. **Passage tone:** Analytical and cautionary. *(Answer: C)*
7. **Which framework facilitates consent-based financial data sharing?** Account Aggregator (AA). *(Answer: A)*
8. **Primary beneficiary of instant digital lending according to paragraph 1:** Digital-first retail consumers. *(Answer: B)*
