Renewable energy transitions in emerging economies have ceased being merely environmental talking points; they are now urgent macroeconomic imperatives. For an energy-hungry economy like India, heavy dependence on imported crude oil and thermal coal exposes the balance of payments to sharp geopolitical shocks and foreign exchange volatility. Over the past five years, unprecedented public-private capital inflows have energized India’s solar and wind generation capabilities, catapulting the country to the fourth rank globally in installed renewable power capacity.

### Directions (Q14 – Q18): Error Detection
14. The governor, accompanied by his senior advisors, (A) / are arriving at the regional headquarter (B) / to review the monetary policy implementation (C) / across public sector branches. (D) / No error (E)  
   *Answer:* **(B)**  
   *Explanation:* When a subject is joined with phrases like "accompanied by", "along with", the verb agrees with the primary subject ("The governor" - singular). Replace "are arriving" with "is arriving".

15. Hard had he entered the conference hall (A) / when the fire alarm began ringing, (B) / causing immediate panic (C) / among the attendees. (D) / No error (E)  
   *Answer:* **(A)**  
   *Explanation:* Correct correlative adverb is "Hardly had he entered..." not "Hard had he".

16. Each of the probationary officers (A) / have submitted their performance reports (B) / before the conclusion of (C) / the orientation workshop. (D) / No error (E)  
   *Answer:* **(B)**  
   *Explanation:* "Each of..." takes a singular verb and singular pronoun: "has submitted his/her performance report".

17. The committee recommended that (A) / immediate measures should taken (B) / to curb the unauthorized transmission (C) / of classified client records. (D) / No error (E)  
   *Answer:* **(B)**  
   *Explanation:* Passive voice modal requires "be": "should be taken".

18. He is senior than me (A) / in terms of corporate service, (B) / yet he respects my opinions (C) / during strategy consultations. (D) / No error (E)  
   *Answer:* **(A)**  
   *Explanation:* Adjectives ending in "-ior" (senior, junior, superior, inferior) take the preposition "to", not "than": "senior to me".

### Directions (Q31 – Q42): Simplification
31. $18.5 \times 24 + 16 \times 25 - 120 = ?$  
   - (A) 724 | (B) 730 | (C) 715 | (D) 740 | (E) 710  
   *Answer:* **(A)**  
   *Solution:* $18.5 \times 24 = 444$. $16 \times 25 = 400$. $444 + 400 - 120 = 724$.

32. $\sqrt{784} + \sqrt{1369} - 25 = ?^2$  
   - (A) 5 | (B) 6 | (C) 7 | (D) 8 | (E) 9  
   *Answer:* **(B)**  
   *Solution:* $28 + 37 - 25 = 40 \approx 36 \implies ? = 6$.

33. $48\% \text{ of } 1250 - 35\% \text{ of } 800 = ?$  
   - (A) 310 | (B) 320 | (C) 330 | (D) 340 | (E) 350  
   *Answer:* **(B)**  
   *Solution:* $0.48 \times 1250 = 600$. $0.35 \times 800 = 280$. $600 - 280 = 320$.

34. $\frac{5}{9} \times 729 + \frac{3}{7} \times 343 - ? = 450$  
   - (A) 102 | (B) 98 | (C) 105 | (D) 108 | (E) 112  
   *Answer:* **(A)**  
   *Solution:* $5 \times 81 = 405$. $3 \times 49 = 147$. $405 + 147 - ? = 450 \implies 552 - ? = 450 \implies ? = 102$.

35. $24^2 - 18^2 + 15^2 = ?$  
   - (A) 477 | (B) 480 | (C) 465 | (D) 490 | (E) 472  
   *Answer:* **(A)**

36. $1440 \div 16 + 840 \div 14 - ? = 110$

39. $36 \times 15 - 28 \times 12 = ?$

### Directions (Q58 – Q65): Arithmetic Word Problems
58. **A and B invest ₹12000 and ₹18000 in a business. Total annual profit is ₹6000. Find B's share:**  
    Ratio $= 12000 : 18000 = 2 : 3$. B's share $= \frac{3}{5} \times 6000 = ₹3600$.
59. **SI on a sum at 8% p.a. for 3 years is ₹1440. Find CI on same sum at 10% p.a. for 2 years:**  
    $P = \frac{1440 \times 100}{8 \times 3} = ₹6000$. CI at 10% for 2 yrs $= 21\% \times 6000 = ₹1260$.
60. **Speed of a boat in still water is 15 km/h, stream speed is 3 km/h. Time taken to travel 72 km downstream:**  
    $D = 15 + 3 = 18 \text{ km/h}$. Time $= \frac{72}{18} = 4 \text{ hours}$.
61. **A train 300 m long running at 54 km/h crosses a pole in:**  
    Speed $= 54 \times \frac{5}{18} = 15 \text{ m/s}$. Time $= \frac{300}{15} = 20 \text{ seconds}$.
62. **Ratio of milk and water in 60 liters is 2 : 1. Water added to make ratio 1 : 2:**  
    Milk $= 40$ L, Water $= 20$ L. $\frac{40}{20 + w} = \frac{1}{2} \implies 20 + w = 80 \implies w = 60 \text{ liters}$.
63. **Cost price of an article is ₹800. Marked at ₹1200. Discount 25%. Profit:**  
    $\text{SP} = 1200 \times 0.75 = 900$. $\text{Profit} = 900 - 800 = ₹100$.
64. **Present age of A is 4 years more than B. 6 years ago, ratio was 3 : 2. Find A's present age:**  
    Difference is constant (4 yrs). 1 unit $= 4$ yrs. Ages 6 yrs ago: A $= 12$, B $= 8$. Present age of A $= 12 + 6 = 18 \text{ years}$.
65. **Area of a circle is 616 sq. cm. Find its circumference:**  
    $\frac{22}{7} r^2 = 616 \implies r^2 = 196 \implies r = 14 \text{ cm}$. Circumference $= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 14 = 88 \text{ cm}$.
