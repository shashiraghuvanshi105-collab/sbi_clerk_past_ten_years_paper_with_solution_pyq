## 1. Master Summary Table: 2010 to 2025

> **Note on SBI Clerk Exam Frequency & Evolution:**
> - SBI Clerk is a recruitment exam conducted according to vacancy requirements. Certain years (**2011, 2013, 2017**) saw **no recruitment notification**.
> - From **2010 to 2014**, the exam followed a **Single-Tier written pattern (200 Marks)**.
> - In **2016**, SBI introduced the modern **Two-Tier Computer-Based Test (CBT)** format: **Preliminary Exam (100 Marks)** and **Main Exam (200 Marks)**.
> - SBI does not release official physical question paper booklets because the exam is an online Computer-Based Test (CBT). Therefore, all papers from modern years are **expert-compiled memory-based question papers** collected from examinees across shifts.

## 2. Examination Pattern Comparison (Single-Tier vs Two-Tier)

### A. Single-Tier Pattern (2010 – 2014/2015)
Candidates appeared for a single written exam of 2 hours 15 minutes:

| Section Name | Number of Questions | Maximum Marks | Time Duration |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 1. General Awareness (Banking, Economy, Current Affairs) | 40 | 40 | Composite Time of 2 Hours 15 Minutes (135 Minutes) |
| 2. General English Language | 40 | 40 | |
| 3. Quantitative Aptitude (Numerical Ability) | 40 | 40 | |
| 4. Reasoning Ability | 40 | 40 | |
| 5. Marketing Aptitude & Computer Knowledge | 40 | 40 | |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | **135 Minutes** |

---

### B. Modern Two-Tier Pattern (2016 – 2025)

#### Phase I: Preliminary Examination (Qualifying in Nature)
Conducted online with sectional time limits:

| Section | Number of Questions | Maximum Marks | Sectional Duration | Medium of Exam |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 1. English Language | 30 | 30 | 20 Minutes | English |
| 2. Quantitative Aptitude (Numerical Ability) | 35 | 35 | 20 Minutes | Bilingual (English & Regional/Hindi) |
| 3. Reasoning Ability | 35 | 35 | 20 Minutes | Bilingual (English & Regional/Hindi) |
| **Total** | **100** | **100** | **60 Minutes (1 Hour)** | — |

*Negative Marking: 0.25 marks deducted per wrong answer.*  
*Sectional Cut-off: There are NO sectional cutoffs; selection is made solely based on overall score.*

#### Phase II: Main Examination (Merit Listing)
Qualifying candidates in Prelims (approx. 10 times the number of vacancies) appear for Mains:

| Section | Number of Questions | Maximum Marks | Sectional Duration |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 1. General / Financial Awareness | 50 | 50 | 35 Minutes |
| 2. General English | 40 | 40 | 35 Minutes |
| 3. Quantitative Aptitude | 50 | 50 | 45 Minutes |
| 4. Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude | 50 | 60 | 45 Minutes |
| **Total** | **190** | **200** | **2 Hours 40 Minutes (160 Minutes)** |
